What this suggests

County diabetes prevalence is strongly associated with inactivity. The pattern remains strong when comparing counties relative to their own state averages. This is an ecological, cross-sectional pattern and does not establish cause or individual risk.

County estimates range from 6.0% to 23.8%, with a median of 10.7%. Food insecurity also has a strong association in the smaller subset with available estimates.

CDC PLACES publishes model-based small-area estimates. State summaries in the accompanying analysis are unweighted averages across available county estimates.
